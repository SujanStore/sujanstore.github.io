How long does delivery take?

Inside Kathmandu Valley, orders are typically delivered within 24–48 hours (free delivery). Outside-valley courier delivery takes 2–4 working days.

About Sujan Store

Sujan Store is a Kathmandu-based boutique dedicated to authentic Nepali ceremonial wear and festive essentials.
